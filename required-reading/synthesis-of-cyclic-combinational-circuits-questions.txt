What was Malik's work on?
He proved that deciding whether acyclic circuit is combinational or not 
is co-NP-complete, and he formulated an analysis algorithm for this task 
based on ternary-valued simulation. He also addressed the issue of
timing analysis and testing for faults.
*Assuming that one doesn't need to specify which paper the question is for
